## Technical Details

1. **Resolution Engine:**
   - [`includes/gumlet-video.php`](file:///c:/Users/Veer/Desktop/office/Refine-new/includes/gumlet-video.php) resolves video slugs via [`assets/data/gumlet-video-map.json`](file:///c:/Users/Veer/Desktop/office/Refine-new/assets/data/gumlet-video-map.json).
   - Video embed endpoint format: `https://play.gumlet.io/embed/{asset_id}`
   - Video thumbnail endpoint format: `https://video.gumlet.io/6553f91b3699cbd2c01ab6a9/{asset_id}/thumbnail-1-0.png`

2. **Frontend Component:**
   - [`includes/treatment-videos.php`](file:///c:/Users/Veer/Desktop/office/Refine-new/includes/treatment-videos.php) renders responsive cards (`tv-card`) with an instant modal player (`#tv-modal`).
